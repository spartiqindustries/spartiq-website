How it works

From drawing to dock.

AI-assisted quoting, supplier matching and production tracking keep lead times short and every order visible. Our engineers review every drawing and sign off every first article.

  1. 01

    Quote

    Drawing reviewed, manufacturability checked, price and lead time returned.

  2. 02

    Qualify

    Supplier matched to the process, material and tolerance, then audited.

  3. 03

    Produce

    First article approved before production; milestones tracked.

  4. 04

    Inspect

    Dimensional, material and visual checks, third-party on request.

  5. 05

    Ship

    Packed, documented and delivered ex-works or landed.
